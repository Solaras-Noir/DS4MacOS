## Questions for the architect

1. `DeviceManager` schedules the IOHIDManager but never calls `IOHIDManagerOpen` (to avoid
   conflicting with a per-device seize). Matching and removal callbacks very likely fire (hidapi does the
   same), but Apple's docs do not say so. Not blocking; step 8 of the Mac protocol checks hot-plug. If it
   fails the fix is to open the manager with the seize option in seize modes.
